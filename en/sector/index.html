Sector

Fire protection in Spain.

We focus on companies that install and maintain active fire-protection systems: a regulated technical-services business with long-standing customer relationships.

Focus

Active fire-protection installers and maintainers.

  • Fire detection and alarm
  • Automatic suppression and sprinklers
  • Hose reels, hydrants and fire-water supply
  • Extinguishers and first-response equipment
  • Periodic maintenance and statutory inspections

We also understand the activities that often sit alongside these businesses, such as passive protection, electronic security and equipment distribution, and take them into account in every transaction.

Approach

How we assess each opportunity.

  • Activity

    The mix of installation, maintenance and repair work, and the nature of the contracts.

  • Customers and team

    The customer base, how recurring the service is, and the technical team that delivers it.

  • Fit

    Which strategic or financial buyers have a clear reason to acquire the company, and why.

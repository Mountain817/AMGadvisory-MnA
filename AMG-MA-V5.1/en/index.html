Mergers & acquisitions · Spain

Mergers and acquisitions in fire protection.

We advise on the sale, acquisition and ownership transition of fire-protection businesses in Spain, with a particular focus on installation and maintenance companies.

Request a confidential conversationOur services

Services

Sales, transitions and acquisitions, in a single sector.

  1. 01

    Sell-side advisory

    Preparing the business, finding buyers and supporting the negotiation of a full or partial sale.

  2. 02

    Ownership transitions

    Assessing the options for an owner's succession or the entry of a partner.

  3. 03

    Counterparty search

    Identifying buyers and investors in Spain and across Europe, and assessing their fit.

  4. 04

    Buy-side advisory

    Finding and assessing companies for sector groups and investors.

Sector

Active fire-protection installation and maintenance.

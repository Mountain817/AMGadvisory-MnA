Our firm

An M&A firm focused on a single sector.

AMG Advisory advises owners, sector groups and investors on the purchase and sale of fire-protection businesses in Spain.

How we work

Working in one sector means we know who buys in it, understand how value is built in a technical-services business, and speak the same language as its owner.

  • Sector focus

    We work with fire-protection businesses and with the groups and investors that acquire in the sector.

  • Preparation before contact

    We analyse the company, its positioning and its potential buyers before any approach to third parties.

  • Discretion and direct contact

    Information is shared only with the owner's authorisation, and clients always work with the same people.

Structure

Part of AMG Advisory AS.

The M&A practice is part of AMG Advisory AS, a company registered in Norway under organisation number 938 271 062, which also provides digital-improvement and automation services to businesses. The M&A team works from Madrid.

The team brings prior experience in corporate and investment banking, financial analysis, and strategy and operations consulting.
